# Privacy Policy for Split Bill

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Split Bill helps you split shared expenses with friends. We built it to collect as little as possible: in fact, we don't collect any of your data.

## What we collect

Nothing. Split Bill has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't run any servers.

## Where your data lives

Your groups, the names you add and your expenses are stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. We can't see or access this data.

## Sharing a group

When you invite people with a link, that group is shared through Apple's iCloud sharing. Anyone who opens the link can join the group and see and edit its members and expenses. You can see who joined, remove people or stop sharing from the group's invite screen ("Manage People & Access"), and people you invited can leave a group at any time.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a group removes it from your devices and your iCloud. Deleting a group you shared removes it for everyone you shared it with. You can also delete all app data from Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.

## Children

Split Bill is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
