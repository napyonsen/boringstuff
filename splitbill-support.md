Need help with Split Bill? Email us at [napyonsen@gmail.com](mailto:napyonsen@gmail.com) and we'll get back to you within 1–2 days.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I invite friends to a group?**
Open the group and tap the person+ button at the top. Send the link with Messages, WhatsApp or any app. Whoever opens it on an iPhone joins the group and can add expenses too.

**My friend opened the link but nothing happened.**
They need Split Bill installed and their iPhone signed in to iCloud (Settings > [your name]). Then they can open the link again.

**Do my friends need an account?**
No. Split Bill uses iCloud, so there's nothing to sign up for. You can also add people by name only, even if they never install the app.

**How does Split Bill work out who owes whom?**
It adds up what everyone paid and what everyone's share was, then suggests the fewest payments that settle everyone up. When someone pays you back, tap Settle to record it.

**How do I leave a group someone shared with me?**
Swipe left on the group in the list and tap Leave.

**Is my data private?**
Yes. Your groups are stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. See our [privacy policy](https://github.com/napyonsen/boringstuff/blob/main/splitbill-privacy.md).
